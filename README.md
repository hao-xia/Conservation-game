This is a simplified model designed for primary and secondary school students to learn the principles of systematic conservation planning. Through this interactive exercise, students explore how to identify optimal conservation strategies that protect the greatest number of species.

This teaching activity is part of the research program SQUEEZE – Arctic Squeezing and Tundra Protection: Opportunities, Planning, and Communication. It was developed by Dr. Hao Xia, with support from Dr. Kerstin Jantke and Prof. Dr. Uwe Schneider.

This teaching material is completely free and open for use by anyone. If you have suggestions or ideas for improving the game, please feel free to contact xiahaoland[at]gmail.com.
